# Meeting Minutes Assistant

An assistant that turns transcripts of in-person meetings into minutes in the secretary's chosen format, with little manual effort.

## The Unique Kernel
The secretary provides a minutes template or prompt, and the assistant uses it to produce complete minutes in that desired format, aiming to need only minimal adjustments.

## Who It's For
Secretaries in companies and other organizations, including trained and amateur secretaries, who prepare minutes for official or informal in-person meetings. Today, they spend time developing minutes manually.

## The Core Loop
The secretary supplies a transcript from an in-person meeting and a desired template or prompt, optionally adding guidance about who said what. The assistant generates minutes in the requested format. The secretary reviews and makes any needed adjustments, then can spend the saved time on other tasks.

## Why This Matters to the Learner
The learner wants to solve the manual meeting-minutes problem, understand how AI agents work, and understand the workflow used to build the solution.

## What "Working" Looks Like
A short on-screen demonstration: a secretary provides an in-person meeting transcript and a template or prompt, the assistant returns complete minutes in the desired format, and only minimal adjustments are needed.

## The POC Boundary
Demonstrate the core transcript-to-minutes workflow for in-person meetings: accept a transcript, accept the user's template or prompt, optionally accept speaker-identification guidance, and produce minutes in the requested format. Keep the experience focused on saving manual time; it is a proof of concept, not a complete platform.
